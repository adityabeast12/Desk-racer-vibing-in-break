# Desk Racer LAN — Competitor & Monetization Research

Nov 1, 2026 · @Aditya Shukla

## Summary

There is an open lane: no polished kart racer owns offline hotspot play. Mario Kart Tour shut down on 29 Sept 2026 with no offline mode, and Mini Militia proved Indians will rally a whole friend group around a local Wi-Fi game (100M+ Play downloads).

The five decisions that matter:

1. **Free to play, cosmetic-only spending.** No gacha, no pay-to-win. Mario Kart Tour's revenue fell sharply after its gacha was removed, and Indian players prefer passes (40%) and cosmetics (37%).
2. **Ads only around solo play, never inside a LAN race.** Smash Karts earns 70–80% from ads placed after a loss; copy the placement, not the frequency.
3. **A season pass ("Desk Pass") as the main earner,** with a free track that progresses fully offline.
4. **"Host shares the party":** content the host owns is playable by everyone in their lobby. One purchase upgrades a whole group, which spreads the game by word of mouth.
5. **Short-video creators over paid ads.** Stumble Guys reached 225M downloads mostly through TikTok creators, not traditional paid installs.

## Competitor teardown

The winners are free, have short sessions and earn money from cosmetics, passes or ads. The one that leaned on gacha shut down.

## What to copy

Copy their assisted controls, short sessions and social hooks; skip their pressure tactics.

- **Assisted controls by default, skill in timing.** Asphalt's simplified [TouchDrive](https://www.pocketgamer.com/asphalt-9-legends/asphalt-9s-stripped-back-controls-are-the-best-way-to-play-the-game-heres-five-r/) steers for you while you time drifts and boosts. Mario Kart Tour paired smart steering with auto-accelerate. Desk Racer: a "Smart" scheme by default, with "Pro" (no assists, purple drifts) and "Tilt" as options.
- **Drift as the core skill.** In KartRider, drifts charge the nitro bar. In Desk Racer, the drift tiers (blue, orange, purple) give the boosts. Make the charge visible on the drift button itself.
- **Short rounds.** Smash Karts matches last 3 minutes, so a 3-lap race should take about 2 minutes. That's the length of a college break.
- **Rotate tracks and run events.** Map rotations kept Smash Karts fresh. Run a weekly featured track with a ghost leaderboard, which works offline and syncs when you're online.
- **Show off in front of friends.** Paints, trails, horns and emotes all show up in the LAN lobby, where your friends see them.
- **Avoid:** red-dot notification spam and stacked subscriptions (a KartRider criticism), gacha (Mario Kart Tour and regulators), and energy timers.

## India market facts

Indian players do pay, but in small amounts. A casual payer spends about $3, so keep prices low and lean on passes and cosmetics.

## Recommended monetization

Primary model: a cheap Desk Pass plus cosmetics, with a one-time Pro upgrade and opt-in rewarded ads. Racing, joining a LAN game and every track in the base game stay free forever.

| Product | Price (INR) | What the player gets | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter Bundle (one per account) | ₹29 | Rare paint + 300 gems + horn | Converts first-time payers cheaply; casual ARPPU is about $3 |
| Desk Pass (6-week season) | ₹99 | 40 premium tiers: bodies, trails, emotes, nameplates; free track for everyone | Passes are the top spend category (40%) |
| Desk Racer Pro (one-time) | ₹149 | No ads, +10% coins, Gold Pencil kart, host up to 8 humans (free hosts: 6) | Mini Militia-style pro pack; the multiplayer perk applies to the host only |
| Track Pack (new cup of 4 tracks) | ₹79 | 4 tracks; playable by the whole lobby when the host owns it | "Host shares the party" growth loop |
| Gem packs | ₹19 / ₹49 / ₹99 / ₹249 | Currency for shop cosmetics | Small sizes match how Indians spend |
| Rewarded ads (online only) | Free | Double coins on results, daily wheel spin, retry the ghost race | Rewarded eCPM is $2.43, the best-paying ad format in India |
| Interstitial (online only) | — | At most 1 per 3 solo races, on results, never for Pro or in LAN | Smash Karts-style placement after a loss; capped to avoid the 38% quit risk |

How offline-first affects payments and ads:

- Purchases need a connection once (Google Play Billing). Cache entitlements locally so owned items work offline.
- Ads load only when online. Preload 2 rewarded ads per AdMob's preloading guidance, and hide the button when none is ready.
- Never put an ad in the LAN flow. A friend group on a hotspot often has no internet anyway.
- The v3 prompt builds this as interfaces with fake billing/ads first. The real SDKs go in once you create the product IDs in Play Console.

## Growth plan

Launch in India through college and hostel friend groups, the same audience that made Mini Militia big.

1. **Closed test (2–3 weeks):** Play Console internal testing with 20–50 friends. Measure races per session, LAN lobbies per day and crash-free rate (target 99.5%+).
2. **Creator seeding:** send early builds to 10–20 small Indian gaming creators on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Moj. Pitch: "4 friends, 1 hotspot, no data needed." Stumble Guys' growth came from creator videos, not paid installs.
3. **Built-in sharing:** Photo mode, a 10-second race replay clip export, and a "Join my race" QR on the host screen. Point friends to Play Store's built-in offline "Share apps" (Nearby Share) for installing.
4. **Weekly events:** a featured track with a ghost leaderboard plus a themed Desk Pass each season (exams, Diwali, IPL, monsoon).
5. **Open beta in India, then wider:** expand to Indonesia, Brazil and Mexico, where hotspot sharing and price-sensitive players are also common.

Launch checklist:

- [ ] Privacy policy URL + Data safety form (no personal data collected offline)
- [ ] Content rating questionnaire (IARC)
- [ ] Store listing: icon, feature graphic, 6+ landscape screenshots, 30 s trailer
- [ ] CC-BY credits on the About screen (already in the asset pack)
- [ ] Release signing key + Play App Signing (the AI Studio project already has a release signingConfig)
- [ ] Product IDs for Pass, Pro, Track Pack and gem packs; AdMob app and ad unit IDs

## Risks and what not to do

- **Employment terms:** check that your employment contract allows you to publish and earn from a personal app before monetizing. The usual safe route is to ship it free first and get written clearance before switching payments on.
- **Trademarks:** don't use "Mario", "Kart" branding, Nintendo-like characters or the Smash Karts name in store text or keywords.
- **Asset licences:** keep the CC-BY credits (Wayfair, ASWF/Ed Mackey, zamdreamer, Darmstadt Graphics Group, hinndia) visible in the app. NVIDIA SimReady datasets each carry their own licence, so check the dataset card before use.
- **Ads in the wrong place** lose players: 38% would quit if there are too many ads. Never during or around LAN races.
- **Gacha and loot boxes:** skip them. They draw regulatory risk, and Mario Kart Tour's revenue fell sharply after its gacha was removed.
- **Online-only dependency:** Mario Kart Tour died with its servers. Desk Racer's offline core is a durable advantage. Keep it.
